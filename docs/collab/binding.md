## Milestone title shapes

- Prototype drafting round: `vX.Y.0bN`
- Trunk release: `vX.Y.Z`

Milestones do not take a package prefix. This repository is the package. The
first prototype drafting round is `v1.0.0b1`. Here the title is a milestone
label only; tags and releases are a later round's decision.

Individuals do not close milestones or cut tags. A reviewer may assign your
issue to a milestone.
